# AI Act Role Assessment v1

- Status: Normative Foundation-governance template
- Contract: [`../architecture/contracts/ai-act-role-assessment-v1.json`](../architecture/contracts/ai-act-role-assessment-v1.json)
- Governing decisions: ADR-001, ADR-005, and ADR-018
- External reference: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)

## Purpose

This is a neutral, versioned evidence template for a future deployment-specific
review of possible EU AI Act operator roles. It is not legal advice and never
assigns `provider`, `deployer`, or any other role from repository facts,
repository visibility, a branch, a package, a name, or a self-report.

At template creation, repository evidence establishes only that Neural Brain is
product- and domain-neutral, has no accepted scoped customer integration, and
does not claim a stable service API, production deployment, external action,
or ready inference deployment. The GitHub repository description and ownership
metadata are not deployment, branding, market-placement, operating-entity, or
release-authority evidence.

## Required deployment record

An accountable owner and qualified reviewer must create one record per
immutable artifact and concrete operating context. Every field in
`assessment_template.required_fields` is mandatory, including independently
verifiable facts about the operating entity, brand or trademark, availability
or putting into service, actual authority over use, jurisdictional nexus,
model/supplier chain, and applicable-law review.

The record must cite the exact artifact digest, intended-purpose contract,
evidence sources, gaps, expiry, and separate release-decision reference.
Candidate role labels are review labels only; they are neither an automatic
classification nor a permission.

## Fail-closed boundary

Missing, unknown, stale, scope-mismatched, or unverified facts make the
assessment incomplete and block that deployment-specific release decision.
The template has no allow path. It cannot create authority, override the
Security Floor, enable a capability, compensate for a delivery-stage or
recognition gate, or replace the independent Protected Control Plane.

Repository and public metadata must never be treated as proof that an artifact
was placed on a market, put into service, operated by a particular entity, or
made available under a particular name or trademark. Those facts require
deployment-specific evidence and qualified review.
