# Future Deployment Subject-Export Review Checklist v1

## Status and boundary

This is a category-only review and audit checklist for the future,
separately-governed S1-14.9 readiness review. It creates no data inventory
fact, discovery, access, processing, export, disclosure, runtime capability,
authority, approval, legal determination, or release outcome.

## Required review record

Record immutable references only, bound to one artifact/deployment, the same
authenticated Tenant/Area/Project scope, purpose, activity, and jurisdiction.
The accountable owner and qualified independent privacy reviewer must record:

- all required categories, including snapshots, as applicable or explicitly
  scope-bound non-applicable;
- all eleven matrix rows, including retention, archive, backup, and recovery;
- lifecycle-specific evidence, review reference, gap/conflict/unknown/
  unavailable-source disposition, and reassessment trigger for every row;
- category-only audit references for lineage, minimisation, third-party rights,
  legal-basis, controller, processor, scope, retention, archive, backup,
  recovery, deletion propagation, and snapshots.

## Fail-closed checklist

- Stop if scope, artifact, purpose, activity, jurisdiction, reviewer, source,
  lifecycle, or evidence currency is unknown, missing, stale, contradictory,
  unavailable, unauthenticated, unqualified, or scope-mismatched.
- Stop if a required category or matrix is absent without an explicit,
  scope-bound qualified-review disposition.
- Stop if a review record contains personal data, identifiers, payloads,
  prompts, contents, credentials, secrets, store locations, or export output.
- Do not access, enumerate, query, copy, inspect, classify from content,
  export, deliver, disclose, redact, delete, restore, or process a store.
- Record only a release stop and escalation; never infer an allow outcome.

## Audit and escalation

The review record must retain immutable category-only references, reviewer
identity evidence, time/currency evidence, and reassessment triggers. Escalate
gaps to the future deployment accountable owner and qualified privacy reviewer.
They must obtain scope-bound facts and review them in a separately governed
process. This checklist cannot authorize any operation or resolve a release
stop.
