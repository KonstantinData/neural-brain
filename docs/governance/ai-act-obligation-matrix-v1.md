# AI Act Obligation Matrix v1

- Status: Normative Foundation-governance template
- Contract: [`../architecture/contracts/ai-act-obligation-matrix-v1.json`](../architecture/contracts/ai-act-obligation-matrix-v1.json)
- Governing decisions: ADR-001, ADR-005, and ADR-018

## Purpose and boundary

This versioned, product- and domain-neutral template records inputs for a qualified future deployment-specific EU AI Act operator-obligation matrix. It separates review-only provider, deployer, model-supplier, downstream-actor, and non-applicability candidates. It does not assign a real role or obligation, determine law, approve a model or supplier, enable deployment or runtime, grant authority, or claim compliance.

The current repository contains no concrete deployment, operator, market placement, supply chain, legal entity, or qualified legal review. The protected Memory Core is an internal subsystem and cannot narrow ADR-018's complete protected cognitive-system product boundary.

## Required evidence and fail-closed review

One immutable record binds artifact/model digest, proposed deployment, authenticated Tenant/Area/Project scope reference, intended-purpose version, external facts, and review snapshot. Every role row requires role-basis and official external-fact evidence, a candidate obligation/article reference, qualified rationale, owner, due date or qualified non-applicability basis, evidence, reassessment trigger, next review date, scope binding, and a gap or release-stop reference.

Unknown roles, facts, owners, due dates, review status, evidence, scope, expiry, contradictions, or reassessment data block deployment-specific release evaluation. The matrix has no allow outcome. It cannot bypass Security Floor, policy, authority, approvals, transition gates, independent verification, evaluation, recognition, delivery-stage gates, or a release decision.

Material artifact/model/supplier, actor, purpose, scope, jurisdiction, legal, evidence, due-date, incident, misuse, or control changes require a separately governed reassessment work item. It preserves prior evidence and never mutates authority, policy, approval, scope, protected state, or runtime access.
