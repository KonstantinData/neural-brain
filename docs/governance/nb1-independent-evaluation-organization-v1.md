# NB-1 Independent Evaluation Organization v1

## Status and boundary

This is a preparation-only organizational contract for a future external EVAL-01 v4 hidden evaluation. It does not appoint anyone, grant authority, accept a candidate, attach hidden material, execute an evaluation, pass a gate, authorize release, recognize Neural Brain, or enable runtime behavior.

The machine-readable authority is [`../architecture/contracts/nb1-independent-evaluation-organization-v1.json`](../architecture/contracts/nb1-independent-evaluation-organization-v1.json). It supplements, and cannot weaken, the preparation boundary in [`nb1-independent-evaluation-preparation-v1.md`](nb1-independent-evaluation-preparation-v1.md), ADR-018, Architecture Directive v4.0, and the Recognition Standard.

## Role appointments and independence

All nine requested roles are named as future responsibilities only: Implementation Owner, Hidden Dataset Provider, Independent Evaluator, Independent Reviewer, Registry Custodian, Key Custodian, Audit Owner, Release Authority, and Recognition Authority. Under the frozen EVAL-01 v4 custody contract, **Hidden Dataset Provider is a logical duty performed by the Independent Evaluator only, not a separately appointable organization or actor**. A separate provider/evaluator organization is prohibited until an accepted v4 revalidation explicitly changes this binding. All other appointment requirements require externally attested identity, organization, reporting line, qualifications, conflict disclosure, mandate, scope, validity, and handoff. Repository identities, subagents, branches, worktrees, process labels, and naming conventions do not prove independence.

The organization contract separates public candidate preparation from the evaluator's combined hidden custody and independent scoring duty, and further separates admissibility review, registry integrity, key custody, audit, release, and recognition. The evaluator remains independent from the Implementation Owner and Independent Reviewer. No actor may use this document to self-appoint, accept its own conflict or evidence, or derive any missing authority.

## Required organizational evidence

Before a role may act, the designated independent-review authority must accept its external attestation and the relevant conflict, custody, and separation records. A deputy needs the same separate acceptance; no role is inherited by availability, reporting line, or handoff alone. Every handoff must bind identities, scope, immutable evidence digests, registry/key status, open exceptions, timestamp, and independent acceptance.

The Independent Reviewer reviews role separation, candidate-freeze admissibility, registry/key records, ledger completeness, and evidence admissibility. The Audit Owner maintains exceptions and checks closure completeness but does not alter technical evidence. Release and recognition remain separate decisions, each requiring its own complete evidence and mandate.

## Matrices and escalation

The contract contains the authoritative RACI, approval, review, deputy, escalation, and decision matrices. All matrices are preparation artifacts: their named future owners do not yet exist in the repository and no matrix entry authorizes an action.

Conflicts, unclear authority, hidden-data leakage, custody loss, key compromise, registry inconsistency, ledger gaps, and post-freeze drift block affected evidence and are escalated according to the contract. Unknown, stale, revoked, self-supplied, scope-mismatched, incomplete, or contradictory organizational evidence is denied.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Every separately appointable role and deputy has external identity, mandate, qualification, conflict, organization, reporting-line, scope, validity, and independence evidence; the v4 Hidden Dataset Provider duty is evidenced through the Independent Evaluator.
- [ ] Implementation Owner cannot hold hidden material, score, approve evidence, release, or recognize.
- [ ] The Independent Evaluator performs the v4 Hidden Dataset Provider duty and is independently separated from the Implementation Owner and Independent Reviewer; no provider/evaluator separation is claimed.
- [ ] Registry and key lifecycle have independent acceptance, revocation, and immutable audit records.
- [ ] Handoffs and escalations are complete and unresolved exceptions block the next decision.
- [ ] Release and recognition are distinct, separately mandated decisions and are never inferred from EVAL-01 preparation or admissibility.
