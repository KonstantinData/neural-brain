# S1-15.2 AI Literacy, Competence Evidence, and Refresh Cycle

## Implementation Evidence

- Task ID: `S1-15.2`
- Objective: Define a versioned, product- and domain-neutral, fail-closed AI
  literacy curriculum, de-identified competence-evidence, and refresh-cycle
  template for developers, operators, approvers, verifiers, support, and
  affected staff.
- Acceptance criteria:
  - [x] Shared and role-specific curriculum topics cover every required role
    category and retain two-plane, gate, scope, privacy, incident, and maturity
    boundaries.
  - [x] Evidence records require version, scope, de-identified reference or
    explicit absence, assessment method, expiry, reassessment, owner, next
    step, and a non-bypass blocker.
  - [x] Competence never creates authority or collapses protected role
    separation; expiry and reassessment fail closed.
  - [x] The template makes no claim about real people, training completion, HR
    personal data, law, runtime authorization, or release.
- Governing ADRs and normative sources: `docs/adr/ADR-001-product-neutral-platform-boundary.md`,
  `docs/adr/ADR-005-hard-security-floor-and-bounded-policy.md`,
  `docs/adr/ADR-018-complete-cognitive-system.md`,
  `docs/architecture/architecture-directive-v4.0.md`, and
  `docs/architecture/neural-brain-recognition-standard.md`.
- Dependencies: `FND-04.9` defines responsibility, approval-authority, and
  independence evidence; this task adds no personnel system, legal conclusion,
  deployment, or runtime control.
- Changed artifacts:
  `docs/architecture/contracts/ai-literacy-competence-evidence-v1.json`,
  `docs/governance/ai-literacy-competence-evidence-v1.md`,
  `docs/traceability/S1-15.2-ai-literacy-competence-evidence.md`,
  `docs/traceability/README.md`, and
  `tests/architecture/test_ai_literacy_competence_evidence_contract.py`.
- Migrations: none; no protected runtime state, identity, authority, policy,
  approval, external effect, release decision, or HR record is added.
- Security and privacy impact: preserves role separation, independent review,
  immutable scope, fail-closed expiry, and de-identified evidence references
  without processing personnel data.
- Open risks: concrete deployment scope, applicable obligations, independently
  governed personnel evidence, qualified review, and separate release or
  operation decisions do not exist in this repository. They remain explicit
  blockers.
