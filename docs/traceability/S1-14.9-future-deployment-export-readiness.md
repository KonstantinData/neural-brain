# S1-14.9 Future Deployment Export Readiness

## Objective and authority

- Task: `S1-14.9` future GDPR subject-export preparation.
- Governing sources: ADR-018, ADR-019, Architecture Directive v4.0, the Neural
  Brain Recognition Standard, and the existing RoPA, data-object, data-flow,
  request, and access-export evidence contracts.
- Delivery: category-only future-deployment inventory, eleven readiness matrices,
  review/audit workflow, fail-closed runbook, and deterministic contract tests.

## Acceptance evidence

- [x] All requested data-class and lifecycle categories are defined without
  asserting they exist in any deployment.
- [x] Coverage, redaction, third-party-rights, legal-basis, controller,
  processor, Tenant-scope, retention, archive, backup, and recovery matrices
  are machine-readable templates with scope, lifecycle, disposition, and review
  evidence fields.
- [x] Immutable authenticated scope, qualified review, data minimisation,
  cross-boundary isolation, explicit unknowns, and reassessment are mandatory.
- [x] The contract and runbook prohibit personal data, discovery, store access,
  processing, export, disclosure, authority, runtime enablement, and release.
- [x] Architecture tests verify all mandatory categories, matrix fields,
  fail-closed semantics, and authority boundary.

## Remaining external blocker

The future deployment accountable owner must provide concrete, immutable,
scope-bound category facts and the qualified privacy reviewer must assess them.
Accepted Protected Control Plane/runtime contracts and independently governed
release evidence are also required before an actual discovery or export can be
considered. This slice creates none of those authorities or capabilities.
