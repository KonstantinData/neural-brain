# S1-14.7 Privacy-Notice Evidence Intake

## Implementation Evidence

- Task ID: `S1-14.7`
- Objective: establish a versioned, product-neutral, fail-closed evidence intake
  for qualified review of possible direct- and indirect-collection privacy-notice
  evidence without adding a privacy notice, legal determination, personal-data
  record, authority, collection, processing, runtime control, or release
  decision.
- Dependencies: S1-14.1 RoPA Evidence Intake, S1-14.2 Personal-Data Flow and
  Recipient Register, and S1-14.3 Article 6 Legal-Basis Evidence Intake as
  prerequisite evidence only; none selects, validates, or establishes a notice.
- Acceptance criteria:
  - [x] The contract requires immutable authenticated Tenant, Area, and Project
    scope; exact artifact, purpose, activity, jurisdiction, and collection-path
    evidence; accountable owner; qualified reviewer; review date; and
    independence evidence.
  - [x] The intake requires qualified-review-only references for direct/indirect
    collection path; controller/representative/DPO contact; purpose/basis,
    categories, data subjects, source, recipients/transfers, retention, rights,
    Article 22 disposition, complaint/contact route, delivery, accessibility,
    and version/integrity evidence.
  - [x] Non-applicability, unknown, conflict, gap, and expiry dispositions are
    explicit, provenance-bound, and never inferred favorably.
  - [x] Missing, stale, contradictory, non-immutable, scope-mismatched, or
    unqualified evidence blocks a deployment-specific release decision.
  - [x] Deterministic tests prohibit legal, notice-sufficiency, authority,
    collection, processing, runtime-enable, maturity, recognition, and release
    claims.
