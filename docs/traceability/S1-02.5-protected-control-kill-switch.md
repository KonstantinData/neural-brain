# S1-02.5: Protected Control Plane Kill-Switch Revalidation

## Scope and status

- Status: Prepared as an unaccepted, evidence-only architecture prerequisite.
- Runtime status: Not implemented; no state store, migration, revocation,
  executor integration, dispatch control, or operational procedure exists.
- Authority status: This prepared evidence does not authorize runtime,
  credential revocation, shutdown, recovery, deployment, or release.
- Governing target: ADR-018, ADR-019, Architecture Directive v4.0.
- Historical input: ADR-006 is superseded/historical and is not implementation
  authority.

## Deterministic repository evidence

`tests/architecture/test_protected_control_kill_switch_contract.py` verifies
that the proposal remains non-authorizing, preserves the required four-state
model, rejects historical reactivation, requires authenticated scope and
separation of duties, enumerates failure behavior, and excludes runtime work.
It does not prove a kill switch works.

## Exact remaining blocker

An authorized architecture decision must accept or replace this proposal. The
decision owner is the Neural Brain architecture decision owner; the contract
owner is the Protected Control Plane architecture owner. Independent
security/safety review and qualified operational recovery ownership must
provide review evidence. Until then every runtime-dependent successor remains
blocked.
